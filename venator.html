Abilities

Consecrating Grenade

Fire a grenade that bounces before exploding, dealing weapon damage and setting enemies on fire. Burning targets deal pure damage to enemies in the area and suffer from reduced healing. A direct hit will detonate on impact without bouncing.

  • Range: 4.5m
  • Cooldown: 25s

Gutshot

Fire a blast with your shotgun, dealing weapon damage and pushing enemies back. Enemies near a wall receive stun and take bonus weapon damage. At max level, stunning an enemy with a wall impact blesses your next heavy melee attack, dealing pure damage and executing low-health targets.

  • Range: 10m
  • Cooldown: 23s

Hex-Lined Snap Trap

Kick a trap that arms after a brief delay. The trap springs on the first enemy it touches, dealing spirit damage, applying immobilize, and revealing enemies for a duration afterwards. Enemies can shoot the trap to disarm it.

  • Range: 2m
  • Charges: 2
  • Charge Delay: 8s
  • Cooldown: 28s

Ira Domini (Ultimate)

Load your crossbow with 3 stakes, dealing massively increased weapon damage. The final stake is blessed, dealing bonus pure damage and executing low-health enemies. Bolts are hitscan and pierce through enemies. At max level, all stakes become blessed.

  • Duration: 15s
  • Cooldown: 160s

Lore

The Venators of St. Benedict are a Vatican-sanctioned order whose members are deployed to regions with a high concentration of supernatural predators. Few in number, their modus operandi is to observe, identify, and exterminate. They are methodical, they are precise, and once they identify a threat to the flock they are relentless.

But even amongst those who are called The Sword Of St. Benedict, one stands apart from the others. A man whose hardened heart has allowed him to pay terrible prices in the name of the greater good… and he has once again been dispatched to New York.

His real name is Father Quinn Rourke, and his mission in the ritual is to kill the Patrons. He refers to himself as a Carnifex—Latin for executioner.
