Solomon cannot zoom in by default, as his right-click is used to move his ability pieces. Projectiles explode in a ~1m radius for full damage, including headshot damage.

Abilities

Setup Bishop

Create a bishop that remains in place indefinitely. Move it to apply a fading slow and deal spirit damage to enemies it passes through. After a delay, it activates diagonal squares that deal additional spirit damage when stepped on. Re-activate to recall all bishops.

  • Range: 20m
  • Cooldown: 12s

Kingside Knight

Promote an ally to be a knight, granting bonus stamina and a barrier for a duration. If self-cast, you develop yourself as king instead, granting double the barrier but no stamina.

  • Range: 28m
  • Cooldown: 25s

Rook Battery

Release a pair of rooks that travel forward, dealing spirit damage and applying immobilize. If the rooks hit a wall, they reflect back in the opposite direction. Re-activate to split the rooks perpendicularly.

  • Cooldown: 33s

Develop Queen (Ultimate)

Summon a queen that crashes down, dealing spirit damage in an area. After a delay, the queen releases squares in straight lines that deal spirit damage again and apply stun. Heroes hit that are low health are checkmated, executing them. Re-activate to recall the queen early.

  • Range: 30m
  • Cooldown: 240s

Innate

Solomon has a passive that alters his aerial movement. He also floats in the air for a few seconds while aiming where to move his pieces.

Lore

In a world that moves too fast, Solomon is content to stop and listen. Offering a friendly ear and good counsel, Solomon has found himself sitting across the chess table from tourists, criminals, and titans of industry. But no matter his opponent, Solomon remains un-phased — because to him, chess is so much more than a game. It's an equalizer. A way for him to connect to people from all walks of life on an even playing field.

As a long-term chess player in Central Park, he joined the Ritual to prevent the power of the Patrons from falling into the wrong hands.
