Mechanic Spotlight: Souls

Mastering the soul economy is the most important skill in Deadlock. Souls act as both your currency for buying items and your experience points (XP) for leveling up abilities.

The Split Mechanic

When you kill an enemy Trooper (lane creep), the souls don't all go to you immediately. Roughly 60% drops as a guaranteed ground orb that auto-collects for your team. The other 40% floats up as a deniable orb.

Securing (Confirming) Orbs

You must shoot or melee the floating orb to secure those extra souls instantly. If you don't, it will eventually auto-collect after a few seconds, but you risk the enemy taking it.

Denying Enemy Souls

This is the crucial skill gap. If an enemy kills a trooper, an orange orb floats up. You can shoot it before they do to steal that 40% for yourself, denying them the souls.

Why it matters: A successful secure + deny is a massive double swing in the lane. It builds your net worth while starving the enemy, often deciding who hits their item power spikes first.
